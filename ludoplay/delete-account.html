Delete your Ludo Play account and data

Ludo Play by BiSoftic · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Option 1 — inside the game (instant)

  1. Open Ludo Play.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Online Account and confirm.

Your online account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — by e-mail (no app needed)

Send an e-mail to bisofticofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Ludo Play account deletion". Include your in-game player name and, if you can, your Friend ID (shown in the game under Profile). If you signed in with Google, tell us. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your online game account (guest or Google link)
  • Profile name, avatar choice, profile photo and country
  • Statistics, rating, level/XP, coins, rewards and dice collection stored online
  • Friends list and friend requests
  • Your entries on leaderboards

What is kept, and for how long

  • Anonymous analytics events (no name or contact details) are deleted automatically after at most 14 months; crash reports after at most 90 days.
  • Advertising data is held by Google under its own policy; you can reset your Advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Data stored only on your phone (settings, offline progress) is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
