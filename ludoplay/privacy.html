Ludo Play — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026
In short: You can play Ludo Play offline without an account. Online play uses a game account (a guest account, or Google or Facebook sign-in where your version offers it) run on Unity Gaming Services. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. We do not sell your personal data. You can delete your online account and its data at any time from inside the game.

1. Who we are

Ludo Play ("the game") is an Android game (com.bisoftic.ludoplay) published by BiSoftic ("we", "us"). Contact: bisofticofficial@gmail.com.

4. Advertising and your choices

The game is free and shows ads (a banner in the main menu and under the board during a match, occasional full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus). Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rating "PG").

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and ads are personalised only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Settings (this button appears in the regions where consent is required). Everywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Analytics

Analytics follow the same consent answer as ads: where consent is required and you decline, no analytics events are sent. Events describe gameplay only (for example "a classic match with 4 players was completed in 7 minutes"); they contain no name, chat, e-mail or other personal content. Ad-personalisation signals are not shared with Google Analytics where consent is required.

6. Children

Ludo Play is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. Online features such as chat and voice are not designed for children. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Online account, profile, statistics and friends: until you delete your online account (in the game, or by asking us).
  • Rooms and lobbies: deleted automatically when the room closes. Chat and voice: not stored.
  • Analytics events: up to 14 months, then deleted automatically. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • E-mails you send us (support, reports): as long as needed to handle them, normally no longer than 12 months.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the game: Settings → Delete Online Account. This permanently deletes your online account, profile, photo, statistics and friends. You can also ask us by e-mail. Step-by-step instructions: Delete your Ludo Play account and data.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before withdrawal). Contact us at bisofticofficial@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Security and transfers

Data sent by the game is encrypted in transit. Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours (including the United States) under their own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the game.
